Fleet Service that comes to you

Fleet service is about consistency more than any single repair. Every truck gets the same checklist, the same logged record, and the same honest scope on what needs to happen now versus next time.

  • Scheduled oil and filter service on a recurring window that fits your operation
  • Brake inspection and pad/rotor replacement when due
  • Battery load testing and replacement before a truck strands a crew
  • Coolant, transmission, and brake fluid service per manufacturer interval
  • Belt, hose, and pulley inspection
  • OBD-II diagnostics for check-engine codes on fleet vehicles
  • Per-vehicle service log: date, mileage, oil grade, fluids topped, items flagged
  • Used oil and filters packed out and recycled

Signs your fleet needs scheduled service

  • Downtime is hurting margins, trucks are sitting at shops instead of working
  • You're piecing together oil changes at parts stores instead of a real maintenance plan
  • Hard to coordinate getting multiple vehicles to a shop at the same time
  • You want one phone number that knows your fleet's history
  • You'd rather have predictable monthly billing than surprise repair bills
  • A driver mentioned a noise but nobody followed up

Why a mobile mechanic makes sense for a small fleet

Shop-based maintenance scales badly for small fleets. Every vehicle is a separate trip, a separate appointment, a separate driver to shuffle around. Mobile flips it: Jack comes to your yard once a month (or whatever cadence fits), services every truck due, and leaves. Your crews never lose a half-day to a service appointment. Trucks stay at the yard, ready to roll the next morning.

SeaBee Jack's is sized exactly for small local fleets, not 200-truck logistics operations, not heavy diesel highway rigs. That focus keeps the relationship personal and the scope honest.

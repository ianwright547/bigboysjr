Service questions

What to know before pickup

Do I need to carry items outside?

No. Tell the team where the items are and include stairs, elevator, hallway, parking, or gate details in the request

What materials require special handling?

Hazardous chemicals, fuels, medical waste, and certain regulated materials may need a specialized disposal provider. Call when you are unsure about an item

Can one pickup include several service types?

Yes. A garage cleanout can include furniture, appliances, boxes, and outdoor items. Load-size pricing may be the easiest place to begin for a mixed job

Is same-day junk removal available?

Same-day service depends on the current route schedule and job size. Send a request early or call to check the earliest available window

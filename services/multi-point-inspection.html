Multi-Point Inspection in Surprise & the West Valley

Every visit, Jack looks the vehicle over and shows you what he sees, so you know what to watch for, with zero pressure to fix it today.

Call (623) 226-3940

A multi-point inspection is the most useful thing you can do for a vehicle you depend on. It's also the perfect job for a mobile mechanic, pure inspection time, no parts, no specialty equipment. SeaBee Jack offers both the standard multi-point look-over (included with every oil change) and a deeper pre-purchase inspection for anyone buying a used vehicle. Done at your driveway in Surprise, at the seller's location, or wherever the car sits anywhere in the West Valley.

Multi-Point Inspection that comes to you

The inspection is a structured walk-around with the vehicle on jackstands. Jack checks each system in order and writes down what he finds, with a clear traffic-light read on each, green (good), yellow (watch), red (needs attention).

  • Tire tread depth measured in 32nds, pressure set to door-jamb spec, wear pattern read for alignment/suspension issues
  • Brakes, pad thickness in millimeters, rotor condition, hose and caliper visual
  • Fluids, engine oil, coolant, ATF, brake, power steering, washer; color, level, smell
  • Belt and hose condition, cracks, glazing, soft spots
  • Battery state-of-health load test and terminal inspection
  • Suspension visual, CV boots, ball joint boots, sway bar links, shock leaks
  • Exhaust system, hangers, rust-through, leaks
  • Lights, headlights, brake, turn, reverse, license plate
  • Wipers and washer function
  • OBD-II scan, current, pending, and stored codes; readiness monitors
  • Written summary you can keep, with photos for anything significant

Signs you need a multi-point inspection

  • You're considering buying a used car and want an honest opinion before signing
  • You're heading on a road trip and want peace of mind before the drive
  • You've moved to AZ recently and want a baseline read on your vehicle in the heat
  • Something feels off but you can't quite name it, sound, vibration, smell
  • You're selling a vehicle and want documentation of its condition
  • It's been a long time since anyone really looked the car over

Why a mobile mechanic makes sense for inspections

Inspections are 100% inspection, no parts to install, no fluids to drain, just an experienced set of eyes going over a vehicle. That's a textbook mobile job. You don't have to schedule a shop appointment for a service you might not even need. Jack comes to where the car is, looks it over, writes it up, and leaves you with the information.

For pre-purchase inspections specifically, mobile is essentially the only option that makes sense. Asking a private seller to drive their car to your shop is a non-starter. Jack meets you at the seller's location, does the inspection right there, and you get a real read before any money changes hands. That alone has saved buyers thousands in deferred maintenance they would've inherited.

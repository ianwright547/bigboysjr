## Signs you need an oil change

- Oil-life percentage on the dash is at or near zero- Maintenance Required or wrench light is on- It's been 5,000 to 7,500 miles since your last synthetic change- It's been 3,000 to 5,000 miles on full synthetic oil- Oil on the dipstick looks black, gritty, or smells burnt- You hear a faint ticking on cold start that fades once oil pressure builds

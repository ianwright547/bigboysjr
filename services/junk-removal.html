Item pricing is useful when you have a short, specific list such as one sofa, a mattress, a refrigerator, and a dresser. Select the closest items in the quote tool, adjust quantities, and add access details.

Prepare for pickup

Make the scope easy to see

Separate the items that are leaving from anything that must stay. Clear a safe path when possible, secure pets, and identify fragile surfaces or building rules. You do not need to carry everything outside unless curbside service is part of your plan.

For appliances, hot tubs, or other connected items, disconnect utilities before the appointment and mention any special removal requirements. For commercial properties, share loading dock access, certificates, parking restrictions, and approved service hours.

Junk removal FAQ

Before your pickup

Can you remove items from inside my home?

Yes. Tell us the room, floor, stairs, elevator, and carry distance so the crew can plan the work

Can I get same-day junk removal?

Same-day service depends on route capacity and job size. Submit the request early or call to check current availability

Do I need to move everything to the curb?

No. Full-service removal includes carrying and loading when access is safe and properly described

What cannot be removed?

Some hazardous, regulated, liquid, or unusually heavy materials require separate handling. Call before booking when you are unsure about an item

How should I estimate a mixed pile?

Use the load-size option and compare the pile with the trailer fractions shown in the quote tool. Add photos or notes when the job is difficult to size

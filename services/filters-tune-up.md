## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should filters be replaced in the West Valley?

Sooner than the owner's manual recommends. Valley dust, haboobs, and monsoon debris load filters up fast. Most West Valley drivers benefit from engine air filter checks every 12,000–15,000 miles and cabin filter swaps every 12 months, both worth pulling and inspecting at every oil change.

### What's the difference between the engine air filter and cabin filter?

The engine air filter cleans the air your engine combusts, a clogged one hurts throttle response and fuel economy. The cabin filter cleans the air your A/C blows at you, a clogged one means weak vents and a musty smell. Both matter, both get checked, and both are usually a 10-minute swap.

### Do you do spark plugs too?

Yes, on most vehicles. Plug intervals run from 30,000 miles on older copper plugs up to 100,000 on iridium. Jack can swap plugs in the driveway when access is reasonable. If a particular engine requires the intake manifold to come off, that's an honest referral.

### Will fresh filters actually help fuel economy?

A heavily clogged engine air filter can shave noticeable MPG and hurt acceleration, especially on smaller engines. It's not a miracle but it's measurable. Cabin filter doesn't affect MPG, but does affect how hard your A/C blower has to work.

### How long does a filter and tune-up visit take?

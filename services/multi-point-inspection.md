## Multi-Point Inspection that comes to you
The inspection is a structured walk-around with the vehicle on jackstands. Jack checks each system in order and writes down what he finds, with a clear traffic-light read on each, green (good), yellow (watch), red (needs attention).

- Tire tread depth measured in 32nds, pressure set to door-jamb spec, wear pattern read for alignment/suspension issues- Brakes, pad thickness in millimeters, rotor condition, hose and caliper visual- Fluids, engine oil, coolant, ATF, brake, power steering, washer; color, level, smell- Belt and hose condition, cracks, glazing, soft spots- Battery state-of-health load test and terminal inspection- Suspension visual, CV boots, ball joint boots, sway bar links, shock leaks- Exhaust system, hangers, rust-through, leaks- Lights, headlights, brake, turn, reverse, license plate- Wipers and washer function- OBD-II scan, current, pending, and stored codes; readiness monitors- Written summary you can keep, with photos for anything significant

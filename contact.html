Contact FAQ

Common questions before pickup

Should I call or use the online quote?

Use the online quote for known items or a mixed pile you can estimate by trailer space. Call for unusual, oversized, commercial, or property-wide jobs

Can I text or email photos?

Email photos and the service address to support@bigboysjr.com. Call first if you need an immediate answer about availability

How quickly will my request be confirmed?

Timing depends on route capacity and the details provided. Complete contact and access information helps the team review the request efficiently

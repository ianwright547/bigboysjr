Why drivers in Sun City choose mobile auto repair

Arizona heat is brutal on a 12-volt battery. The internal plates corrode faster, the electrolyte cooks off, and a battery that would last five years in Ohio gives up after about two-and-a-half here. That's not a sales pitch, that's chemistry. Jack runs a load test on every battery he sees and will tell you flat out if it still has life in it or if it's about to leave you stranded in the Safeway parking lot.

The second reason Sun City residents love mobile service is simpler: they don't want to drive to a shop, sit in a plastic chair for two hours, and then drive home. Jack handles it in the carport while you have coffee. For households where one spouse no longer drives, this is the version of car maintenance that actually works.

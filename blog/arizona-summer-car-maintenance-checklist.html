Surviving Arizona Heat: A Summer Car Maintenance Checklist

June 28, 2026 · SeaBee Jack's

Arizona summers punish vehicles in ways most of the country never sees. Triple-digit heat stresses every system in your car, and the desert is an unforgiving place to break down. This checklist keeps you cool and on the road.

1. Cooling system first

Heat is hard on your radiator, hoses, and coolant. An overheating engine is the number-one summer breakdown. Make sure coolant is full, fresh, and mixed correctly, and that hoses aren't soft or cracked.

2. Don't trust an old battery

It's a myth that batteries only die in winter. Extreme heat actually shortens battery life faster than cold by evaporating the internal fluid. If yours is a few years old, get it tested.

3. Check tire pressure in the morning

Hot pavement plus underinflated tires is a recipe for a blowout. Check pressure when tires are cool and inspect for cracking from UV and heat.

4. Service your A/C

In Arizona, air conditioning is safety equipment, not a luxury. If it's blowing warm or weak, a quick check can find a low charge or a failing component before you're stuck sweating.

5. Protect your fluids

Oil breaks down faster in extreme heat. Staying on top of oil and other fluids keeps everything lubricated and cool when it matters most.

What Items Can Be Removed?

A simple way to sort common household items before you begin a junk-removal quote.

Disposal guide•4-minute read

Most junk-removal jobs begin with familiar household items: furniture that no longer fits, an old appliance, a mattress, a garage pile, or debris from a project. Grouping the items before you start makes it easier to select a pricing method and share the right details.

Common removal categories

Furniture and household items

Couches, chairs, tables, dressers, bookcases, bed frames, and general household clutter are common pickup items.

Appliances and electronics

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, and similar household equipment should be identified individually so the pickup can be planned correctly.

Mattresses and bedroom items

Mattresses, box springs, frames, and bedroom furniture are easiest to price when you list the major pieces together.

Outdoor and project debris

Bagged yard waste, branches, outdoor clutter, removed fixtures, packaging, wood, and other construction debris can often be part of a broader pickup plan.

Make a quick item list

Start with the biggest pieces, then look in closets, garages, sheds, and outdoor storage for the small items that should be included. If you have a short list, item pricing is usually the clearest route. For a mixed pile, load-size pricing may be faster.

Unsure about a specific, oversized, or restricted item? Call before booking. It is better to confirm the plan than to guess.

Tell the crew about special access

Stairs, elevators, tight gates, parking, and long carrying distances are useful details to share. They help the crew prepare and help make the estimate more meaningful.

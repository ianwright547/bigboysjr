How to declutter your garage fast.

Start by opening up a clear walking path and grouping items by what stays, what can be donated, and what needs removal. You do not have to make every disposal decision before getting a quote.

Work in zones

Begin with the easiest visible section, then move through shelves, floor piles, and storage corners. Put large removal items where they are easy to identify.

Price the pickup

Use an item list for specific pieces, or choose load-size pricing if you have a mixed garage pile. Mention access details when you request pickup.

Terms of service

Important details about estimates, pickup requests, access, material, and confirmation

These terms describe the general rules for using the Big Boys Junk Removal website, requesting an estimate, and arranging a pickup. By using the site or sending a request, you agree to provide accurate information and to review the confirmed scope before work begins.

Estimates are starting points

Prices shown by the online estimator are starting estimates based on the items, quantities, load size, labor selections, access details, and other information submitted. An estimate is not a guaranteed quote or a confirmed appointment. The final scope and price are confirmed after Big Boys reviews the request and before the crew begins work.

Changes to material, quantity, weight, access, stairs, disassembly, distance, route timing, disposal requirements, or site conditions may change the confirmed total. If the job is materially different from the submitted description, the team may pause and review the new scope with you.

Pickup requests and confirmation

Submitting a form, sending an email, or calling does not create an appointment until Big Boys confirms availability, service area, scope, and timing. A preferred date or time window is a request, not a promise that a crew is reserved.

Please keep the contact phone available around the requested window and respond to questions about parking, gates, elevators, building hours, pets, or access. If another person will meet the crew, that person should have authority to identify what is approved for removal.

Customer responsibilities

You are responsible for giving an accurate address, describing the items and access conditions, identifying anything that must stay, securing pets, removing valuables and personal documents, and obtaining any required property-management, landlord, association, street, or building permissions. Customers should not move unsafe or unusually heavy material solely to prepare for pickup.

Items should be accessible and safe to handle unless the confirmed scope says otherwise. Big Boys may decline work when conditions create an unreasonable risk to people, property, equipment, or the environment.

Material and prohibited items

Common approved material may include furniture, mattresses, appliances, household clutter, yard debris, office contents, and non-hazardous renovation material. Acceptance depends on condition, size, weight, safety, route, and disposal options.

Hazardous chemicals, fuels, paint, medical waste, explosives, ammunition, compressed cylinders, radioactive material, biohazards, and other regulated items may require a specialized provider. Do not hide restricted material inside bags, boxes, appliances, furniture, or a mixed pile. Ask before booking when you are unsure about an item.

Access, labor, and property care

The customer must disclose stairs, elevators, narrow paths, long carries, fragile surfaces, damaged steps, low clearances, parking restrictions, gates, and building rules. Big Boys will use reasonable care while performing the confirmed work, but cannot be responsible for conditions that were not disclosed or for pre-existing damage that is unrelated to the crew’s work.

Disassembly, same-day priority, heavy-item handling, and other labor selections may carry additional charges when shown in the estimate or confirmed scope. The crew may stop or reschedule when weather, unsafe access, blocked property, or changed conditions prevent safe service.

Communication and cancellations

Contact the team as soon as possible if the address, scope, access, or timing changes. Availability may change when a request is delayed, a customer cannot provide access, or the property is not ready. Any cancellation, rescheduling, or deposit terms that apply to a specific job will be communicated with that job confirmation.

Website content and contact

Website copy, photos, service descriptions, and pricing examples are provided for general planning and may change as routes, disposal options, and operations change. Big Boys works to keep information useful and current but does not promise that every page is complete for every property or material type.

Questions about these terms or a specific pickup can be sent to support@bigboysjr.com or discussed by phone at (470) 660-6874.

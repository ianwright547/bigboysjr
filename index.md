## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you really come to me?

Yes. Jack brings the oil, filters, and tools to your driveway, your work parking lot, or wherever the vehicle sits. No drop-off, no waiting room, most oil changes are done in about 15 minutes.

### Will you leave an oil stain on my driveway?

No. Jack lays down containment mats and drip protection before any work starts, and packs out and recycles the used oil and filter. Your driveway looks the same when he leaves as when he arrived.

### What kind of oil do you use?

Your choice of full synthetic only, plus a quality filter to match. In Arizona's heat, full synthetic is usually the smart call, and Jack will tell you what he'd run in his own truck.

### What else can you do besides oil changes?

It's "& More" for a reason: brakes, batteries, air and cabin filters, fluid service, starters, alternators, sensors, and general repair.

### What areas do you cover?

Surprise and the West Valley: Sun City, Sun City West, Peoria, Glendale, El Mirage, Litchfield Park, Goodyear, Waddell, Wittmann and more. Outside the area? Call and ask, we can often make it work.

### How does pricing & payment work?

You get a real, upfront price before any work starts, estimates over the phone are free. Cash or card, and Jack goes over the total with you before he begins, so there are no surprises.

### How long does a mobile oil change take?

Most full-synthetic oil changes are done in about 15 minutes, start to finish. Jack lays down containment mats, drains the old oil, swaps the filter, refills to spec, resets the oil-life monitor where applicable, and does a quick under-vehicle look before packing up.

### Do I need to be home while you work?

Not necessarily. As long as the vehicle is accessible and the keys are arranged, Jack can do the work while you're at the office, the gym, or running errands. He texts a "done" message and the total before he leaves.

### What vehicles do you work on?

Cars, trucks, SUVs, and most light-duty work vehicles, gas or diesel. Half-ton, three-quarter-ton, and most one-ton pickups are no problem. Heavy commercial diesel rigs are case by case, give Jack a call with the make and model.

### How soon can you get here?

Most West Valley calls get same-day or next-morning service. In Surprise, response time is often within the hour during business hours. Call (623) 226-3940 with your zip and Jack will give you a real time window.

### Do you offer a warranty on the work?

Yes. Labor is warrantied 90 days or 4,000 miles, whichever comes first, on the specific repair performed. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, which is typically 12 to 24 months on filters, batteries, brake pads, and most other components we install.

### Can you handle a small fleet for my business?

Yes. Scheduled on-site oil changes and routine maintenance for local fleets, landscape crews, contractors, delivery, and mobile service operations. We schedule a recurring window and bill per vehicle or monthly. Call (623) 226-3940 to set up a fleet account.

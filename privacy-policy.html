Privacy policy

How Big Boys handles information shared through the website and quote process

Big Boys Junk Removal respects the information customers share while asking about service. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when you contact us.

Information you choose to provide

When you request an estimate or pickup, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, pickup address, preferred date, time window, notes, item list, load size, and access details. You may also send photos or other job information by email or phone when asking for help with a larger or unusual project.

You do not need to submit sensitive personal information, financial account numbers, passwords, medical information, or government identification through the quote form. If a job requires information beyond normal scheduling details, ask the team how it should be handled before sending it.

How information is used

Big Boys uses submitted information to respond to pricing questions, estimate the scope of a removal job, confirm route availability, coordinate access, communicate about a pickup, answer customer-service questions, and maintain a basic record of the request. Information may also be used to improve the wording, usability, and reliability of the website and quote experience.

Online pricing is a starting estimate, so the team may use the details you provide to clarify material, labor, access, timing, and final scope before work begins. We do not use the quote form to make automated credit or insurance decisions.

Website technology

The website may use ordinary browser storage, server logs, analytics, and similar technical information to keep pages working, understand general traffic, protect the service from abuse, and improve performance. Technical data may include a device type, browser, approximate region, referring page, requested URL, and time of visit. It is not intended to identify you personally on its own.

Sharing and service providers

Big Boys may share necessary scheduling information with crew members, property contacts, communication providers, hosting providers, analytics services, or other vendors that help operate the website and fulfill the requested service. Those parties receive only information reasonably needed for their role.

Big Boys does not sell customer contact details as a standalone product. Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect the safety of customers or workers, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transition involving the website or service operations.

Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to a request, complete a service relationship, keep business records, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, and maintain security. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, but Big Boys uses reasonable administrative and technical practices for the systems under its control.

Your choices

You may ask what contact information is associated with a recent request, ask for a correction, or request that a non-required marketing communication stop. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, safety, legal, or operational reasons. To ask a privacy question, email support@bigboysjr.com or call (470) 660-6874.

Children and policy updates

The website is intended for adults arranging property services and is not directed to children. Big Boys may update this policy when the website, service process, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with its effective date reflected by the latest published content.
